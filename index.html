Meet SIRKAL

A leader raised by Karachuonyo, ready to serve all of Karachuonyo

Jeff Ongoro — known to the people as SIRKAL — has served the people of Kanyaluo Ward as their Member of County Assembly in the Homa Bay County Assembly. He rose from humble beginnings and built his politics on the ground: at funerals, in churches, at women's group meetings and on the village paths of Karachuonyo.

As MCA he has opened roads, brought water, improved healthcare and schools, built ECDE centres and helped ECDE teachers find jobs. Now he is asking for your vote to take that same hands-on leadership to the National Assembly, carrying the voice of every ward of Karachuonyo to Parliament on the MDG ticket.

The SIRKAL Agenda 2027–2032

Ten commitments for Karachuonyo

Each pillar comes with concrete actions through NG-CDF, national budget lobbying, and partnerships with the County Government of Homa Bay.

Roads & Connectivity

Every market, school and health centre reachable in every season.

  • Lobby KeRRA and KURA to tarmac key feeder roads linking Kendu Bay, Kosele, Pala and the lake beaches
  • Murram and grade village access roads ward by ward, with culverts and bridges at flood points
  • Street lighting in trading centres for safer evening business
  • Public roads scorecard published every quarter

Healthcare for All

Quality care close to home, with medicines on the shelf.

  • Push for upgrading dispensaries to fully equipped health centres, with maternity wings
  • Champion SHA registration drives so no family is left uncovered
  • Support Community Health Promoters with kits and fair stipends
  • Ambulance and boat-ambulance access for lakeside communities

Free Primary Education for All

Truly free schooling. No child sent home over levies.

  • Use NG-CDF to build and repair classrooms, so parents aren't charged development fees
  • Push for timely release of capitation funds to schools
  • Modern latrines and clean water in every primary school
  • Sanitary pads programme so girls never miss class

Free Day Secondary for All

Every child who completes primary school gets a secondary school seat.

  • Expanded, transparent NG-CDF bursaries with the list of beneficiaries published
  • Full scholarships for top-performing needy students
  • Labs, libraries and ICT rooms in our day secondary schools
  • Support for TVETs so young people learn job-ready skills

Youth Employment

Real jobs and real skills, starting today through ServiceHub Kenya.

  • Partnership with ServiceHub Kenya Limited: youth register free, list their skills and get hired across Homa Bay County
  • Mentorship in arts, entrepreneurship and personal growth
  • Help youth groups access Youth Fund, Hustler Fund and county funds
  • Digital skills hubs for online work

Agriculture & Fisheries

Turning our land and our lake into income.

  • Subsidised certified seed and fertiliser reaching every ward on time
  • Small-scale irrigation to end dependence on rain
  • Fish cooling and landing facilities at our beaches
  • Cooperatives and market linkages so farmers get fair prices

Food Security

No family in Karachuonyo should go hungry.

  • Drought-resistant crops and grain-storage support for households
  • School feeding programmes in all public primary schools
  • Emergency relief that is fast, fair and openly listed
  • Kitchen-garden and poultry programmes for women's groups

Security

Safe homes, safe markets, safe roads.

  • Build and equip police posts through NG-CDF where they are needed
  • Strengthen community policing and Nyumba Kumi
  • Lighting and patrols at markets and beaches
  • Rehabilitation and opportunity programmes that steer youth away from crime

Care for Our Elders

Dignity for those who built Karachuonyo.

  • Register every eligible elder for Inua Jamii cash transfers
  • Free medical camps and eye clinics for the elderly
  • SHA coverage for vulnerable older persons
  • Support for widows, orphans and persons with disabilities

Clean Water & Sanitation

Safe water within walking distance of every home.

  • Boreholes, water pans and lake-water treatment points
  • Piped water extensions to trading centres
  • Modern latrines in schools and public spaces
  • Water committees run by the community
